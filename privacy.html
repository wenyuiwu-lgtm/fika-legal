隱私權政策

適用於「Fika 咖啡廳」手機 App(以下簡稱「本 App」)。最後更新:2026 年 10 月 28 日。

1. 我們是誰

本 App 由獨立開發者(以下簡稱「我們」)製作與營運,是一款學習瑞典語的咖啡廳經營遊戲。任何隱私問題請寄信到 wenyuiwu@gmail.com。

2. 我們蒐集哪些資料

  • 帳號資料:使用電子郵件註冊時的電子郵件與(加密後的)密碼;以「訪客」身分遊玩時,系統會建立一組匿名帳號 ID,不需要任何個人資料。
  • 你設定的公開資料:暱稱、咖啡廳名稱、頭像、玩家 ID。這些資料會顯示給你的好友,並可能被知道你玩家 ID 的其他玩家搜尋到。
  • 遊戲與學習資料:金幣與寶石、咖啡廳等級與擁有的物品、單元與關卡進度、答題紀錄、收藏的單字、連續學習天數、使用的語言設定。
  • 社交資料:好友關係與邀請、好友聊天訊息(最多 80 字)、足跡留言(最多 30 字)、禮物、拜訪與「戳一下」的紀錄、你封鎖的玩家,以及你送出的檢舉(含被檢舉那則內容的文字快照)。
  • 技術與錯誤資料:當 App 發生錯誤或當機時,我們使用 Sentry 收集錯誤報告,內容包含錯誤訊息、裝置型號與作業系統版本、App 版本,以及你的匿名帳號 ID(不包含電子郵件)。

我們不會使用麥克風、相機、通訊錄或精確位置;目前沒有廣告,也沒有用於追蹤你的第三方行銷工具。若日後加入廣告或應用程式內購買,我們會先更新本政策並依平台規定徵求你的同意。

3. 我們如何使用資料

  • 提供遊戲與學習功能、儲存並同步你的進度;
  • 提供好友、聊天、留言、送禮等社交功能;
  • 偵測並修復錯誤、維護服務安全、防止濫用與作弊;
  • 處理檢舉與客服信件,並遵守法律義務。

4. 資料如何儲存與分享

你的資料儲存在雲端資料庫服務 Supabase;錯誤報告由 Sentry 處理;App 的程式更新透過 Expo 提供。這些服務商只依我們的指示處理資料。除此之外,我們不會出售你的個人資料,也不會與第三方分享用於廣告。

你的暱稱、頭像與咖啡廳資訊,會依遊戲功能顯示給你的好友(例如好友的咖啡廳預覽、聊天與留言)。請勿在暱稱、咖啡廳名稱或訊息中填寫個人敏感資料。

5. 保存期限

我們在你的帳號存在期間保存你的資料。你刪除帳號後,你的遊戲資料、好友與聊天紀錄會從我們的資料庫中刪除。已送出的檢舉紀錄會保留(但與你的帳號脫鉤),以便處理申訴與維護社群安全。錯誤報告依 Sentry 的預設期限自動清除。

6. 你的權利與刪除帳號

  • 刪除帳號:在 App 內「設定 → 刪除帳號」即可立即刪除帳號與所有資料,無需聯絡我們。注意:此登入帳號同時也用於 svenska-app 字典,刪除後該帳號與資料也會一併刪除。步驟說明見客服與刪除帳號頁。
  • 查閱、更正、匯出:暱稱與咖啡廳名稱可在 App 內修改;如需取得你的資料副本或有其他請求,請寄信至上述信箱,我們會在合理期限內回覆。
  • 依你所在地的法規(例如歐盟 GDPR、台灣個人資料保護法),你可能擁有查詢、更正、刪除、限制處理、資料可攜與反對處理的權利,也可向當地主管機關申訴。

7. 兒童

本 App 適合 13 歲(含)以上使用者。我們不會刻意蒐集 13 歲以下兒童的資料;若發現,將盡快刪除。家長若認為孩子提供了資料,請來信聯絡我們。

8. 資料安全

我們使用加密連線傳輸資料,並以存取權限控制保護資料庫。但沒有任何系統能保證絕對安全,請妥善保管你的密碼。

9. 政策變更

我們可能更新本政策,重大變更會在 App 內或此頁面公告,並更新上方日期。

Privacy Policy

Applies to the “Fika Café” mobile app (the “App”). Last updated: 28 October 2026.

1. Who we are

The App is created and operated by an independent developer (“we”). It is a café-management game for learning Swedish. For any privacy question, email wenyuiwu@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and (hashed) password if you sign up with email. If you play as a “guest”, we create an anonymous account ID and need no personal information.
  • Profile you choose to show: nickname, café name, avatar and player ID. These are shown to your friends and may be found by players who know your player ID.
  • Game and learning data: coins and gems, café level and owned items, unit and level progress, answer history, saved words, study streak, language setting.
  • Social data: friendships and requests, friend chat messages (up to 80 characters), guestbook messages (up to 30 characters), gifts, visits and “poke” records, players you block, and reports you send (including a text snapshot of the reported content).
  • Technical and error data: when the App errors or crashes we collect error reports with Sentry: error message, device model, OS version, App version and your anonymous account ID (not your email).

We do not use your microphone, camera, contacts or precise location. There are currently no ads and no third-party marketing trackers. If we add ads or in-app purchases in the future, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the platform requires it.

3. How we use data

  • To provide the game and learning features and save/sync your progress;
  • To provide social features: friends, chat, messages and gifts;
  • To detect and fix errors, keep the service secure and prevent abuse and cheating;
  • To handle reports and support emails, and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Storage and sharing

Your data is stored with the cloud database provider Supabase; error reports are processed by Sentry; app updates are delivered through Expo. These providers process data only on our instructions. We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties for advertising.

Your nickname, avatar and café information are shown to your friends as part of game features (for example a friend’s café preview, chat and messages). Please do not put sensitive personal information in your nickname, café name or messages.

5. Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your game data, friendships and chat history are deleted from our database. Reports you submitted are kept (detached from your account) to handle appeals and keep the community safe. Error reports are removed automatically after Sentry’s default retention period.

6. Your rights and deleting your account

  • Delete your account: in the App go to “Settings → Delete Account” to immediately delete your account and all data, without contacting us. Note: this login is also used for the svenska-app dictionary, so that account and its data will be deleted as well. See the Support & account deletion page.
  • Access, correction, export: you can edit your nickname and café name in the App. To get a copy of your data or make another request, email the address above and we will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU GDPR or Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act) you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

7. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 and will delete it promptly if we learn of it. Parents who believe their child has provided data should contact us.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections and access controls to protect the database. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password safe.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page and the date above will be updated.
